Relay Arcade is an original, compact party-console experiment inside Tien OS. Host **Catch the sparks** with a four-character room code, then steer with the keyboard, touch controls, or a controller tab.

The first version is intentionally serverless: room play uses browser messaging between same-origin tabs in one browser profile. It stays playable solo when no second tab is available; tabs on other devices cannot join.
